2. General

TheCrazyBot ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that can be added to Discord servers ("guilds") by server administrators. This privacy policy explains what data the Bot processes as part of its operation, for what purpose, on what legal basis, and for how long.

The Bot only processes data provided via the Discord API, as well as data generated through the use of Bot features (e.g. level points, ticket messages). Discord itself, as an independent controller, additionally processes data under its own Discord Privacy Policy — this policy only covers processing carried out by TheCrazyBot.

  • Message Content Intent — for prefix commands, ticket transcripts, and the moderation/audit-log feature.

4. Retention period

  • User profiles (level, economy, statistics): kept as long as you're active on at least one server running the Bot, or until you delete them yourself via /privacy delete-my-data.
  • Ticket transcripts: stored encrypted as long as the server keeps using the Bot, or until the owner deletes them via /privacy delete-server-data, or the Bot is removed from the server.
  • Moderation log content (deleted/edited messages): not permanently stored, only processed temporarily for display in the respective server's log channel.
  • Server data: fully deleted as soon as the Bot is removed from the server, or at the owner's request.

7. Your rights as a data subject

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority

How to exercise these rights

For access, export, and deletion of your own data, you don't need to contact us separately:

Command Effect
/privacy view-my-data Overview of your data in Discord, plus a full copy as a JSON file
/privacy delete-my-data Permanently deletes your entire user profile (confirmed via a button)

Server owners additionally get an overview or a full deletion of all bot data belonging to their server via /privacy view-server-data and /privacy delete-server-data.

For anything else (rectification, restriction, objection, general questions), reach us via the contact details in section 1.

8. Data security

Ticket message content is stored encrypted in the database (AES encryption). Access to the database is restricted to Bot operation. Despite all security measures, complete security of data transmitted over the internet cannot be guaranteed.

9. Automated decision-making / profiling

No automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place that produces legal effects or significantly affects you. Level, economy, and statistics values serve entertainment purposes within the server only.

10. Use by minors

The Bot is not specifically directed at children under 13, or the minimum age applicable in your country under the Discord Terms of Service. Discord's own age restrictions apply to the use of Discord and, by extension, the Bot.
